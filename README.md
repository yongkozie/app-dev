## Introduction
**Attack on Titan** is a Japanese anime series about humanity fighting for survival against giant creatures called Titans.

## Why I Like It
I like this anime because:
- It has an amazing storyline.
- It has unexpected plot twists.
- The characters have interesting backgrounds.
- The animation and fight scenes are incredible.
